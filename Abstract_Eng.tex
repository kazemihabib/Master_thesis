This package provides an unofficial LaTeX template designed to comply with the formatting and typographic guidelines established by the Alma Mater Studiorum – Università di Bologna (UniBO). It is intended to assist students, researchers, and faculty in producing consistently formatted academic documents — including theses, reports, and dissertations — in accordance with the university's editorial standards.
The template encapsulates the required document structure, covering the title page (frontespizio), body layout, bibliography, and appendices. It enforces the prescribed typographic conventions: A4 page size, standard margins of 2.5 cm on all sides, recommended font families (such as Times New Roman, Arial, or equivalent), a body font size of 12–13pt, 1.5 line spacing, and footnotes set in 10pt. The frontespizio follows the official UniBO facsimile, featuring the institution's full Latin name — Alma Mater Studiorum · Università di Bologna — along with fields for the degree programme, thesis title, candidate, supervisor, and academic year.
